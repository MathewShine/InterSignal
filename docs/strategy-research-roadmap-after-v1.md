# Strategy Research Roadmap After V1

## Phase A — Required infrastructure

Build a realistic Indian-market transaction-cost/slippage/tax model, freeze a temporal holdout and later walk-forward harness, and establish point-in-time intraday execution data. Sector and catalyst history follow when their timestamp integrity can be audited.

## Phase B — Controlled hypothesis experiments

Run at most one isolated dimension per authorization. The first candidates are slot-capacity distortion, one architecture-led R:R mapping, and temporal replication. Freeze ranking before signal changes. No threshold mining or simultaneous ranking/signal modification.

## Phase C — Validation

Develop only on the predeclared development window and evaluate once on the untouched validation window. Add expanding-window walk-forward replication after parameters are frozen. Apply realistic costs and report source, admitted, congestion, and yearly behavior.

## Phase D — Strategy V2 candidate consideration

Consider a Strategy V2 candidate only after a falsifiable hypothesis survives chronology, costs, sample requirements, temporal validation, and a separate promotion review. Command 06 creates no Strategy V2.

## Phase E — Paper-trading graduation

Only a separately approved, validated candidate may enter paper research. Live or small-capital deployment remains outside this roadmap until fill quality, monitoring, operational controls, and independent graduation criteria are established.
