# Post-research strategy program review V1

## Executive decision

InterSignal completed Strategy Discovery Families A–G with `COMPLETE_NO_VALIDATED_STRATEGY`. The review selects `NEXT_PROGRAM_PRIMARY_DIRECTION = PRODUCT_PLATFORM_PROGRAM` and `NEXT_PROGRAM_SECONDARY_DIRECTION = FORWARD_DATA_PROGRAM`.

`STRATEGY_RESEARCH_PROGRAM_STATUS = PRODUCT_PLATFORM_FOCUS` and strategy discovery is `PAUSED`. This is a governance selection only. Neither programme has started, Strategy V2 remains `NOT_CREATED`, and Family H remains `NOT_PLANNED`.

## What A–G accomplished

The cycle produced useful evidence even though it did not validate a strategy. Family A showed strong development performance, realistic INR 500,000 whole-share implementation, and independent control reproduction, but its formal validation was inconclusive and its corrected post-outcome evidence was unsupportive. Family C preserved a reusable compression signal without producing a portfolio candidate. Families D and F identified specific data and licensing blockers. Families B, E, and G supplied negative evidence that prevents further investment in weak or redundant designs. CAP4’s failed holdout and Family A’s CA defect reinforced the importance of independent temporal validation, causal prehistory, and auditable pipelines.

No strategy was validated because development evidence did not generalize cleanly, the only formal Family A run was compromised by an implementation defect, corrected Family A evidence was unsupportive but not pristine, and no other family produced a validation-ready candidate.

## Four programme options

### Option A — Forward data

Scientific value and cleanliness are high because genuinely new observations can restore unseen evidence. Overfitting risk and direct licensing cost are low. The principal drawback is time: a meaningful unseen sample must accrue naturally. Family A may be tracked only as a `FROZEN_RESEARCH_BENCHMARK`, and Family C only at signal level. Any observation would be `RESEARCH_SHADOW_MODE`, not deployment rehearsal or paper-trading readiness.

This is the secondary direction. It should be considered for separate authorization after shadow governance and data-lineage requirements are complete.

### Option B — Data infrastructure

Continuous intraday data would support execution research, microstructure, entry timing, future families, and execution-quality analytics. Authorized catalyst data would support Research, fundamental/catalyst intelligence, portfolio alerts, event risk, and future Invest/Research modules.

Reuse and optionality are high, but engineering effort, licensing dependency, cost, and time-to-decision are also high. The option is deferred to targeted feasibility work; no provider or acquisition is authorized.

### Option C — New hypothesis

A genuinely new economic or behavioral mechanism could have research value, but no distinct, independently preregisterable, data-ready mechanism currently exists. Continuing automatically to Family H would force strategy production instead of following evidence. This option is `NOT_YET` justified and remains deferred.

### Option D — Product/platform

Product/platform work offers durable value even if no strategy-alpha programme succeeds. Research workbench capabilities, signal registry, auditability, data lineage, portfolio intelligence, risk monitoring, analytics, and decision support strengthen InterSignal while retaining future research options.

This is the primary direction. It has high engineering effort but low licensing dependency, low overfitting risk, high reuse, high optionality, and high independent product value.

## Why the primary direction was selected

Product/platform work provides the highest immediate value independent of alpha success and directly addresses lessons from A–G: evidence traceability, pipeline parity, data quality, decision governance, risk visibility, and portfolio intelligence. It also provides the clean foundation needed for a later research-shadow programme without confusing observation with deployment readiness.

The decision would change if a meaningful unseen dataset accumulated for a preregistered frozen benchmark, reusable licensed intraday or catalyst data became feasible, a distinct data-ready economic hypothesis emerged, or platform engineering opportunity cost proved greater than its cross-module value.

## Three-month decision programme

Month 1 defines the product programme charter, product-owner priorities, module boundaries, governance constraints, and non-goals across Research, Portfolio OS, risk, auditability, signal registry, and data lineage.

Month 2 reviews architecture and resource tradeoffs, assesses research-shadow governance, and performs bounded feasibility assessment for Family D/F data infrastructure without selecting vendors or acquiring data.

Month 3 makes the programme go/no-go decision, sequences any separately authorized work, and decides whether passive forward observation should be activated. No implementation is authorized by this review itself.

## Six-month objectives and decision gate

Months 1–3 complete the charter, architecture, governance, and resource decisions. If separately authorized, months 4–5 establish reusable research auditability, lineage, portfolio intelligence, and shadow-observation foundations without live deployment.

At month 6, InterSignal reviews demonstrated product value, genuinely unseen forward-data accumulation, D/F feasibility, and the quality of any new economic hypothesis. The explicit decision is to continue the platform programme, validate eligible forward evidence, change programme direction, or pause alpha research temporarily.

## Resources, success, and stop conditions

Engineering effort is high; data effort, compute/storage, and manual review are medium; near-term licensing/data cost is low because acquisition is excluded.

Programme success means an approved charter and module sequence, complete lineage and auditability requirements, a governed shadow-observation design ready for separate authorization, explicit D/F feasibility decisions, useful research/portfolio/risk capabilities independent of alpha, and a clean evidence-based next decision. No financial-return target is defined.

Pause or stop alpha research if non-generalization persists, data quality remains insufficient, licensing burden is excessive, no distinct economic hypothesis emerges, engineering opportunity cost becomes too high, or the product owner does not confirm platform value.

## Product roadmap interaction

Research, Portfolio OS, and Data Layer are primary beneficiaries. Invest is secondary through future catalyst intelligence and portfolio decision support. Broker Connection is design-only, with no broker calls or execution. Trade remains deferred because no validated strategy exists.

No new strategy, validation run, paper-trading programme, live-trading programme, data acquisition, or infrastructure implementation was started by this review.
