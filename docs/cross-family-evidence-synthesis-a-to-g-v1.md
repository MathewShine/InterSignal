## What worked

Family A supplied the strongest strategy-level evidence. The frozen INR 500,000 implementation grew to INR 955,331.6799 in DEVELOPMENT, a +91.06633598% net return, 24.10585067% CAGR, and 22.92216992% maximum-drawdown magnitude after the full frozen cost model. All three DEVELOPMENT calendar years were positive and 8 of 10 completed net portfolio-rebalance periods were positive. Family G independently reproduced those exact control metrics.

The INR 500,000 implementation materially reduced whole-share and capital distortion seen at INR 100,000. It had no unaffordable-name instances and invested 97.351% on average, although tracking differences were not eliminated. This capital is an implementation-fidelity reference, not an alpha input or live-capital recommendation.

Two narrower findings remain reusable without becoming candidates. Family C's `EDGE-EVIDENCE-C-COMPRESSION-001` showed that pre-breakout compression of at most 8% improved event-level quality. Family F's bounded NSE corporate-announcement pilot demonstrated 95.24% trusted timestamp quality and 100% canonical linkage, stable document identity, and reproducible retrieval. The former is signal-level evidence; the latter is data-infrastructure evidence.

## What failed

CAP4 is the central validation-discipline lesson: promising DEVELOPMENT behavior failed its one-shot holdout and ended `DOES_NOT_GENERALIZE` / `REJECTED`. Development strength alone cannot promote a strategy.

Family B's tested positive-return filter was redundant, while the apparent SMA200 advantage was primarily explained by historical-data availability and became weak and sparse after remediation. Family C's 1.5x volume treatment and tightest-compression-first capacity ranking failed. Family E's pullback/reclaim architecture was nonviable after costs, and its SMA50 structure rule removed a descriptively better cohort. Family G's exact quarterly NIFTY 500 close-above-SMA200 all-in/all-cash gate missed two profitable control quarters, materially reduced return, and worsened maximum drawdown. These conclusions are limited to the frozen definitions and do not justify universal claims against absolute momentum, volume, SMA50, SMA200, or regime research.

## What was blocked

Family D was not performance-evaluated. Exact prior-20 intraday continuity reached 3,752 of 4,821 targets (77.826177%), leaving 1,069 incomplete and missing the frozen >=95% gate. It is data blocked, not strategy failed.

Family F was not performance-evaluated. The technically preferred route is authorized NSE corporate-announcement historical delivery, an equivalent licensed structured feed, or an official alternative passing the same quality and permission gates. Public visibility is not authorization for systematic historical collection. It is source blocked, not strategy failed.

## Cross-family lessons

The current evidence provisionally favors a simple standalone architecture over the tested large overlays. That is not a universal proof that multi-factor strategies cannot work: it records that Family A's simple architecture survived the current DEVELOPMENT programme while the tested B, C, E, and G additions did not add reliable executable edge.

Implementation is part of the hypothesis. Whole shares and capital scale changed fidelity; capacity prevented event evidence from translating cleanly in Family C and constrained Family E; transaction costs materially affected every executable conclusion. Point-in-time membership, historical lookback availability, continuous intraday coverage, timestamp causality, and acquisition licensing must be governed before performance is interpreted.

The original 60–65% win-rate aspiration is not a selection rule. Family A is a periodic portfolio architecture. Its available success observation is 8 of 10 completed net rebalance periods (80%), explicitly a portfolio-rebalance-period statistic rather than a trade-level win rate. No trade-level Family A win rate is invented or used for selection.

## Candidate gate and freeze

`MOM-A-002` plus `A2-002` passes the ten gates because it is executable and positive after frozen costs; no known data artifact explains the result; INR 500,000 materially controls implementation distortion; the three available years are positive; no methodology or data blocker prevents a validation design; the architecture and artifact lineage are frozen; the exact candidate has not failed validation; and no candidate-specific validation outcome drove tuning.

The candidate is incorporated strictly by frozen reference. It preserves point-in-time Nifty 500 membership, the frozen price and liquidity gates, 6M ranking, top-decile selection, quarterly equal-weight rebalancing, INR 500,000 capital, whole shares, frozen cost accounting, and the existing next-open execution semantics. It adds no Family B filter, Family C compression overlay, Family G regime gate, or other cross-family hybrid.

Families B–G produce no alternative validation candidate. B, C, E, and G fail the positive executable-evidence gate for their tested architecture or treatment; D and F were not performance-evaluated.

## Validation caveats

The main risks are the three-year DEVELOPMENT window, regime concentration, only ten completed quarterly periods, selection-breadth shifts, turnover and cost sensitivity, and dependence on the INR 500,000 whole-share implementation assumptions.

The project has previously seen aggregate diagnostics over later periods in other contexts, so formal Family A validation is governed/sealed but cannot be described as philosophically pristine laboratory-grade unseen data.
