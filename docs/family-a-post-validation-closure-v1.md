# Family A post-validation closure V1

## Final governance decision

`FAMILY_A_VALIDATION_CANDIDATE_STATUS = CLOSED_NOT_ADVANCED` and `FAMILY_A_STRATEGY_V2_CANDIDACY = REJECTED_FOR_CURRENT_CYCLE`.

The original formal record remains exactly `FAMILY_A_FORMAL_VALIDATION_STATUS = INCONCLUSIVE`. It is not changed to `FAIL`: the formally governed run was compromised by an implementation defect that truncated causal prehistory at the validation boundary. That defect created false global corporate-action eligibility failures in the first three intervals.

The root-cause audit established `IMPLEMENTATION_LOGIC_DEFECT`. The code-only remediation restored the required 126-session causal prehistory while leaving the strategy, CA rules, costs, schedule, performance window, and sealed criteria unchanged. Structural verification returned `FIX_VERIFIED_STRUCTURALLY`.

The exact frozen `MOM-A-002 / A2-002` candidate was then evaluated in a separate evidence class after the original outcomes were known. That evaluation returned `POST_OUTCOME_REMEDIATED_RESULT = FAIL`, `POST_OUTCOME_REMEDIATED_GENERALIZATION_INDICATION = UNSUPPORTIVE`, and `POST_OUTCOME_STRATEGY_V2_REVIEW_STATUS = DOES_NOT_SUPPORT_CANDIDATE_REVIEW`.

This is not described as a pristine validation failure. The formal validation remains inconclusive; the current governance conclusion combines that formal state with unsupportive post-outcome evidence and is `FAMILY_A_CURRENT_GENERALIZATION_CONCLUSION = INSUFFICIENT_SUPPORT_FOR_ADVANCEMENT`.

## Evidence retained

Family A’s positive development evidence is preserved. The INR 500,000 whole-share implementation produced a 91.06633598% net development return, 24.10585067% CAGR, and 22.92216992% maximum drawdown over 2022–2024. Family G independently reproduced the ungated control behavior, capital realism was demonstrated, and the quarterly momentum architecture remains historically useful research evidence.

Those findings are classified `HISTORICALLY_STRONG_DEVELOPMENT_EVIDENCE_NOT_GENERALIZED`; they are not erased merely because Family A is not advanced.

The later-period evidence is registered as `EDGE-NEGATIVE-A-LATER-PERIOD-GENERALIZATION-001`. Under corrected CA semantics, net return was -3.62326400%, CAGR was -2.438579367208471%, maximum drawdown was 23.859516337847389%, and three of six primary intervals were positive. Its status is `POST_OUTCOME_REMEDIATED_EVIDENCE`.

Strong development performance therefore did not provide sufficient evidence of stable later-period generalization. This is descriptive evidence and remains subject to the permanent post-outcome caveat.

## No-salvage and holdout policy

`FAMILY_A_POST_VALIDATION_TUNING = PROHIBITED_ON_CURRENT_HOLDOUT`. The known period must not be reused to test 3M, 9M, or 12M momentum; monthly rebalancing; different selection percentiles; changed price or liquidity gates; SMA, compression, or regime overlays; different costs; or different capital.

`FAMILY_A_2025_2026_HOLDOUT_STATUS = CONTAMINATED_FOR_FUTURE_MODEL_SELECTION`. The outcomes are known. This period remains available for diagnostics and documentation only and cannot be represented as unseen evidence for future candidate selection.

The desired 60–65% win rate remains an aspiration, not a threshold or selection target. No strategy may be modified solely to chase a desired win rate.

## Research lessons

Validation integrity depends on causal prehistory and data-pipeline parity between development and validation. Truncating feature or eligibility history at a holdout boundary can create false eligibility failures that obscure the candidate’s actual behavior.

After that defect was corrected, the later-period evidence did not retain the development edge: development CAGR was 24.10585067%, while the post-outcome remediated CAGR was -2.438579367208471%.

Future model-selection validation requires a new temporally unseen period. A possible source is forward or paper observation after the current research freeze, but no future dates are defined or authorized here.
