## Authorization and licensing gate

`FAMILY_F_LICENSE_GATE_V1` blocks historical acquisition until at least one condition is satisfied:

1. Written or contractual authorization exists for reproducible historical NSE announcement delivery.
2. An approved licensed provider supplies equivalent timestamp and document fidelity.
3. Another official source independently passes the same quality gates and permits historical acquisition.

Public webpage visibility is not permission for systematic multi-year automated collection. Anti-bot, rate-limit, authentication, licensing, or other access controls must not be bypassed. This closure contacted no source or vendor and purchased nothing.

It requires at least 95% trusted publication timestamps, canonical linkage, stable document identity, and reproducible retrieval, plus immutable provenance, understood revision/duplicate handling, and explicit authorization or licensing.
