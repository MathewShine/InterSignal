# Strategy Family A literature notes V1

## Scope and interpretation

These notes record external motivation for `STRATEGY_FAMILY_A_MOMENTUM_V1`. They are not InterSignal empirical results and do not guarantee profitability in Indian equities. Family A remains a development-only research family until its preregistered baselines are evaluated by a later command.

## Research basis

- Medium-term cross-sectional momentum: Jegadeesh and Titman documented relative performance persistence using formation and holding horizons in the three-to-twelve-month range. This supports testing medium-term winner ranking as a distinct family, not importing Strategy V1 breakout rules. Source: [Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers: Implications for Stock Market Efficiency](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1993.tb04702.x).
- Conventional skip-month momentum: the Kenneth R. French Data Library defines momentum portfolios from prior returns ending before the most recent month. Family A's `12M_EX_LAST_1M` uses exact trading-session endpoints—252 sessions of history with the most recent 21 sessions excluded—rather than calendar approximations. Sources: [Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html) and [Momentum Factor details](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/det_mom_factor_daily.html).
- Indian broad-universe momentum design: NSE Indices publishes momentum strategy indices using medium-term price momentum. The Nifty200 Momentum 30 framework uses 6- and 12-month momentum measures, while the Nifty500 Momentum 50 establishes the relevance of a broader Indian equity universe. Family A deliberately tests simpler raw cross-sectional returns, equal weighting, and point-in-time Nifty 500 membership; it does not reproduce either index. Sources: [Nifty200 Momentum 30](https://www.niftyindices.com/indices/equity/strategy-indices/nifty200-momentum-30-index), [Nifty200 Momentum 30 white paper](https://www.niftyindices.com/docs/default-source/indices/nifty200-momentum-30-index/nifty200_momentum_30_index_whitepaper_sep_20.pdf?sfvrsn=3eb99734_4), [Nifty500 Momentum 50](https://www.niftyindices.com/indices/equity/strategy-indices/nifty500--momentum--50), and [NSE Indices equity-index methodology](https://www.niftyindices.com/Methodology/Method_NIFTY_Equity_Indices.pdf).
- Rebalance frequency and turnover: NSE Indices' 2026 momentum study discusses quarterly versus semiannual rebalancing and reports higher churn for more frequent rebalancing in its studied construction. Family A therefore preregisters monthly and quarterly variants of the same 6-month signal and treats turnover as a primary future metric. It does not infer that either frequency will win. Source: [Momentum Strategy white paper 2026](https://www.niftyindices.com/docs/default-source/indices/nifty500-momentum-50/momentum-strategy-whitepaper_2026.pdf?sfvrsn=4f4d6335_6).
- Indian evidence: an Indian-equity study of relative and absolute momentum supplies local-market motivation, but its liquid equity-futures scope is not identical to Family A's long-only cash-equity universe. It is contextual evidence only. Source: [Momentum in Indian Equity Markets](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/SSRN_ID3375873_code2374856.pdf?abstractid=3345280&mirid=1).
- Broader relative-strength evidence: cross-asset research documents common value and momentum effects across markets, supporting relative ranking as a research concept while not establishing Family A's profitability. Source: [Value and Momentum Everywhere](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2174501).
- Transaction-cost sensitivity: implementation costs can materially change anomaly returns. Family A therefore freezes the existing nonzero cost model and requires gross and cost-adjusted reporting in the later performance command. Source: [Trading Costs of Asset Pricing Anomalies](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~afrazzin/pdf/Trading%20Cost%20of%20Asset%20Pricing%20Anomalies%20-%20Frazzini%2C%20Israel%20and%20Moskowitz.pdf).
- Momentum crashes and regimes: momentum can suffer episodic severe losses. Family A records that caveat but does not add a regime filter in this baseline command. Source: [Momentum Crashes](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439.pdf).
- Absolute or trend confirmation: time-series momentum provides motivation for a later, separately preregistered relative-plus-absolute branch. It is explicitly excluded from Family A Command 01. Source: [Time Series Momentum](https://fairmodel.econ.yale.edu/ec439/mosk.pdf).

## Architecture consequences frozen before results

The literature motivates a small design set rather than a parameter sweep. The only authorized baselines are 6-month monthly, 6-month quarterly, and 12-minus-1-month monthly. All use top-decile relative ranking, equal weights, long-only cash equities, and next-session-open execution. Generic 3M, 9M, and raw 12M calculations exist for shared infrastructure and data inspection only; they are not registered experiments.

Absolute momentum, volatility scaling or filtering, market-regime conditioning, fundamentals, no-trade bands, stops, targets, and alternate portfolio breadths remain potential future controlled branches. None is part of Command 01.
